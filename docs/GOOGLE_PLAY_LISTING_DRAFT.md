# Google Play Listing Draft

## App name

Broke to Millionaire

## Short description

Build a life from nothing through work, study, business, and smart choices.

## Full description

Start with no home, no job, and no savings. Every decision shapes the life you build.

Find work, manage your energy, study for better careers, and balance monthly income against housing, food, transport, health, loans, and family costs. Save carefully, improve your skills, and unlock new opportunities as your net worth grows.

Build your path:

- Create and save your own character.
- Choose housing, meals, transport, healthcare, and personal purchases.
- Develop career skills through education and work experience.
- Apply for jobs and climb technology, finance, and other career ladders.
- Start companies, hire employees, and manage business growth.
- Trade fictional stocks and coins in a simulated market.
- Build relationships and make long-term life choices.
- Face health, stress, financial, and random life events.
- Earn monthly income while paying realistic recurring expenses.
- Continue limited progress while away from the game.
- Retire and begin a new legacy with permanent bonuses.

Broke to Millionaire is a fictional life-and-money simulation. All cash, investments, casino activity, rewards, and purchases shown inside the game are virtual and have no real-world monetary value. Virtual currency cannot be withdrawn, transferred, or exchanged for real money or prizes.

The game contains optional rewarded advertisements and may offer in-app purchases after store configuration is complete.
